Hockey Score Widget shows the live score of an NHL game in OBS.

Before installing
- Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit.
- OBS Studio 28 or newer, with its WebSocket server turned on (Tools > WebSocket Server Settings).
- An internet connection to get the scores.

Your data
- Everything stays on your computer: no account, no ads, no data sent to the author.
- The software only reads public scores from the NHL API (api-web.nhle.com) and its logos (assets.nhle.com).
- Your settings are saved in %APPDATA%\HockeyScoreWidget. Uninstalling the software keeps them.
